Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our giving plans, tax benefits, and impact.

Are my donations tax-deductible?

Yes,Groww Charity is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 12-3456789). All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive a donation receipt for tax purposes immediately after your donation is processed.

Can I change or cancel my giving plan?

Yes, you can change your giving plan or cancel at any time. Simply log into your donor portal or contact our donor support team. Changes made before the 25th of the month will take effect for the following month's donation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. For annual giving plans or large donations, we also accept checks and stock transfers.

How do you ensure 91% goes to programs?

We maintain rigorous financial controls and undergo annual independent audits. Our low overhead is achieved through volunteer support, in-kind donations, and strategic partnerships. View our complete financial reports on our website.

Can I designate my donation to a specific program?

Yes! When you sign up for a giving plan, you can choose to support a specific program area: Education, Clean Water, Healthcare, Economic Empowerment, or Where Most Needed. You can change your designation at any time through your donor portal.

Do you offer corporate matching programs?

Yes, we work with hundreds of companies that match employee donations. Check with your HR department to see if your company has a matching gift program. We can help facilitate the matching process.
