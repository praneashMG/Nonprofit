Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to our most commonly asked questions below.

You can donate online through our secure donation portal, via bank transfer, or by mailing a check to our headquarters. We accept one-time donations, monthly recurring donations, and planned giving options.

91% of every dollar donated goes directly to our programs. Only 9% is used for administrative costs and fundraising, which is well below the industry average of 15-25%.

We offer both local and international volunteering opportunities. You can apply through our volunteer portal, join our local chapters, or participate in our global mission trips. Virtual volunteering options are also available.

Yes,Groww Charity is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. You will receive a tax receipt for your records.

We welcome corporate partnerships! Please contact our partnerships team at partnerships@Groww Charity.org or call (555) 123-4567 ext. 101. We offer various partnership levels including sponsorship, employee matching, and cause marketing opportunities.
