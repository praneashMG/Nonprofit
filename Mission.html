Our Purpose & Vision

Our Mission: Creating Lasting Change

We exist to empower underserved communities worldwide through sustainable development, education, and healthcare initiatives that address root causes and create self-sufficient futures.

Our Mission Statement Our Core Values

Our Mission Statement

"To empower underserved communities worldwide by creating sustainable solutions that address the root causes of poverty, inequality, and injustice through education, healthcare, economic development, and community partnership."

Groww Charity Foundation

Founded in 2010

Our Vision

A world where every person has access to the resources, opportunities, and support needed to build a self-sufficient, dignified, and hopeful future.

Vision Goals by 2030

Provide quality education to 1 million children in underserved communities

Ensure clean water access for 500,000 people in water-scarce regions

Create sustainable livelihoods for 100,000 families through economic empowerment

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These fundamental principles guide every decision we make and every action we take in pursuit of our mission.

Community Partnership

We work alongside communities, respecting local knowledge and empowering them to lead their own development.

Sustainable Impact

We design programs for long-term success, ensuring communities can maintain progress independently.

Integrity & Transparency

We maintain the highest ethical standards and provide full accountability for our actions and finances.

Inclusive Empowerment

We prioritize the most marginalized, ensuring everyone has a voice and opportunity to thrive.

How We Work

Our Approach to Change

We follow a proven methodology that ensures sustainable, community-led transformation.

1

Community Assessment & Partnership

We begin by listening. Our teams work directly with community leaders and members to understand their unique challenges, strengths, and aspirations.

"We don't assume we know what a community needs. We ask, we listen, and we partner."

2

Sustainable Solution Design

Together with the community, we co-create solutions that are culturally appropriate, environmentally sustainable, and economically viable.

"The best solutions are those that communities can maintain long after we're gone."

3

Local Capacity Building

We invest in local leadership and skills development, training community members to implement, manage, and sustain projects independently.

"Our goal is to work ourselves out of a job in every community we serve."

4

Impact Measurement & Adaptation

We continuously monitor progress, measure impact, and adapt our approach based on data, feedback, and changing circumstances.

"What gets measured gets improved. We're committed to evidence-based impact."

Our Journey

Our Story & Milestones

From humble beginnings to global impact, our journey is one of growth, learning, and transformation.

2010

Foundation Established

Groww Charity was founded by Dr. Maria Rodriguez and James Chen with a mission to address educational inequality in rural Guatemala.

2013

First Clean Water Initiative

Expanded our mission to include clean water access, installing our first 50 wells in communities across Kenya.

2016

Healthcare Programs Launched

Introduced mobile healthcare clinics and maternal health programs in partnership with local health authorities.

2019

Global Expansion

Reached our 25th country of operation and surpassed 50,000 lives directly impacted through our programs.

2022

Digital Transformation

Launched digital literacy programs and virtual volunteering platform to expand our reach and impact.

2025

Current & Future Goals

Working toward our 2030 vision goals while continuously innovating our approach to maximize sustainable impact.
